## What Does 'Furnished' Really Mean in Athens?

Understanding the local market is key. The term 'furnished' can vary, but there are common expectations when searching for apartments in Athens.

### What is included in a furnished apartment in Athens?

A typical furnished apartment in Athens is move-in ready. You can expect to find:

- **Major Furniture:** Bed with mattress, sofa, dining table and chairs, wardrobe, and basic storage units.
- **Major Appliances:** Refrigerator, washing machine, oven/stovetop, and air conditioning units.
- **Basic Kitchenware:** Often includes pots, pans, plates, glasses, and cutlery.
- **Amenities:** Sometimes includes a television, microwave, and basic linens.

The level of quality and completeness varies, so always confirm a detailed inventory list before signing a lease. These furnished apartment amenities athens provide significant upfront savings and convenience.

### Unfurnished Apartment Expectations in Athens

An unfurnished apartment expectations athens scenario is quite different. In Greece, 'unfurnished' often means completely empty. It is common for these apartments to lack:

- Light fixtures in some rooms.
- A refrigerator or washing machine.
- Wardrobes or closets (these are often considered furniture).

This bare-bones approach gives you a blank canvas but requires a significant investment in time and money to make it livable.

## Pros and Cons: Which Option is Best for You?

Making the best option furnished or unfurnished athens choice depends on your personal circumstances, budget, and long-term plans.

#### Furnished apartment

**Pros**

- Lower upfront costs and financial risk.
- Move-in ready, ideal for expats and students.
- No hassle of buying, moving, or selling furniture.
- Predictable total cost for shorter stays.

**Cons**

- Higher monthly rent.
- Less personal style and customization.
- Potential liability for damaging owner's furniture.

#### Unfurnished apartment

**Pros**

- Complete freedom to design your own space.
- Lower monthly rent.
- You own the furniture as a long-term asset.
- More cost-effective for stays of 3+ years.

**Cons**

- Extremely high initial investment.
- Logistical challenge of shopping and assembly.
- Difficult to manage for temporary stays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much more expensive are furnished apartments in Athens?

Furnished apartments in Athens are typically 20-30% more expensive than their unfurnished counterparts. For a one-bedroom apartment that might rent for €750 unfurnished, you can expect to pay around €950-€1000 for a furnished version in the same building or neighborhood.

### What is the average cost to furnish an apartment in Athens?

The average cost to furnish a one-bedroom apartment in Athens from scratch is between €4,000 and €6,000. This includes essential furniture (bed, sofa, table), major appliances (fridge, washing machine), kitchenware, and other basic necessities.

### Is it cheaper to rent a furnished or unfurnished apartment in Athens for one year?

For a one-year lease, it is significantly cheaper to rent a furnished apartment. Our analysis shows that despite the higher monthly rent, you could save over €2,000 in the first year by avoiding the massive upfront cost of purchasing furniture and appliances.

### What is typically included in a furnished apartment in Athens?

A standard furnished apartment in Athens includes all major furniture (bed, sofa, table, wardrobe), major appliances (fridge, oven, washing machine), and often basic kitchenware and utensils. However, it's always best to get a detailed inventory list from the landlord before signing the contract.
