Cine Alimos of Alimos
Cine Alimos of Alimos
Cine Alimos, located in the seaside suburb of Alimos, offers a unique outdoor cinema experience in Athens. This open-air venue provides a perfect setting for enjoying movies under the stars, with the sound of the waves and a stunning view of the Aegean Sea in the background. Whether you’re a local resident or a visitor, Cine Alimos invites you to relax in a beautiful, natural environment while watching classic and contemporary films.
The cinema features comfortable seating and a range of refreshments, including traditional Greek snacks and drinks. With its proximity to Alimos Beach, you can easily combine a day at the beach with an evening movie for a complete entertainment experience. Cine Alimos is a must-visit destination for film lovers seeking a memorable and authentic Greek summer evening.

 Highlights of Cine Alimos 
Unique Open-Air Cinema Experience
Cine Alimos offers an unforgettable outdoor cinema experience, where you can watch films under the stars, surrounded by the sound of the Aegean Sea.
Scenic Coastal Location
Situated right along the Alimos coast, the cinema provides stunning views of the sea, creating a relaxed and picturesque atmosphere for moviegoers.
Proximity to Alimos Beach
Cine Alimos is conveniently located near Alimos Beach, allowing visitors to enjoy a full day of beach activities before heading to the cinema for an evening film.
Easy Access to Public Transport
The venue is well-connected via public transport, with nearby tram and bus stops providing convenient access for visitors from different parts of Athens.
Nearby Restaurants and Cafés
The area around Cine Alimos features a variety of dining options, from casual beachside cafés to traditional Greek tavernas, perfect for a pre-movie meal or post-show drink.
Family-Friendly Atmosphere
The cinema offers a welcoming and safe environment for families, with a variety of films suitable for all ages and ample seating to accommodate groups.
Comfortable Seating and Refreshments
Cine Alimos provides comfortable seating options and a snack bar offering classic cinema treats as well as local Greek snacks and drinks.
Close to Other Attractions
With its location in Alimos, the cinema is also close to other attractions like the Alimos Marina and local parks, making it an ideal spot for a well-rounded day of entertainment.
 Cine Alimos Tour - Itinerary 
Arrival at Alimos Beach
Begin your day at Alimos Beach, just steps away from Cine Alimos. Relax on the sandy shores, swim in the clear waters, or enjoy beachside activities like volleyball and paddleboarding.
Sunset Stroll Along the Promenade
Take a leisurely walk along the Alimos promenade as the sun sets, providing stunning views of the Aegean Sea. Stop by the nearby cafés for a refreshing drink or snack.
Dinner at a Local Taverna
Head to a nearby taverna for a traditional Greek dinner. Enjoy local specialties like grilled seafood, moussaka, and fresh salads.
Movie Screening at Cine Alimos
Arrive at Cine Alimos for your open-air cinema experience. Find your seat and settle in as you enjoy a film under the stars, with the gentle sea breeze and sounds of the waves.
Departure
Your guide will assist in organizing transportation back to your accommodation or help you explore other nightlife options nearby.
 Local Facilities for Cine Alimos Tour 
Beach Access
Cine Alimos is conveniently located near Alimos Beach, allowing visitors to enjoy sunbathing, swimming, and water sports before their cinema experience.
On-Site Snack Bar
Cine Alimos offers a snack bar serving classic movie treats like popcorn, as well as local Greek refreshments and drinks, ensuring a delightful experience.
Nearby Restaurants and Taverns
Visitors can explore various dining options in the vicinity, including beachside cafés and traditional Greek taverns, perfect for enjoying a meal before or after the movie.
Parking Facilities
For those arriving by car, public parking spaces are available near the cinema, making it convenient for visitors driving to the venue.
Proximity to Alimos Marina
Just a short walk away, Alimos Marina offers additional leisure options, including boat rentals and waterfront bars, ideal for extending your evening activities.
