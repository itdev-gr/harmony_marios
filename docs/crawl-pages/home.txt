Frequently asked questions
We understand that planning your stay or managing your property may come with questions. Here are some of the most common inquiries
 How do I book an apartment? 
Booking is easy! Simply browse our listings, select your preferred property, and fill out the booking form with your details. Our team will confirm availability and finalize your reservation.
 Can I customize my tour itinerary? 
Absolutely! We offer personalized tour services, allowing you to customize your itinerary based on your interests and schedule.
 What is included in the Airbnb management service? 
Our Airbnb management covers everything from preparing your property to managing guest communication, check-ins, and maintenance, ensuring a seamless experience for both you and your guests.
 How do I start a renovation project with Harmony Rental? 
Contact us for a consultation where we’ll discuss your needs, budget, and timeline. Our team will create a tailored renovation plan and manage the project from start to finish.
 What types of boats are available for rental? 
We offer a variety of boats, including yachts, sailboats, and speedboats. Choose the option that best fits your preference and enjoy an unforgettable maritime experience.
 Are there any discounts for long-term stays? 
Yes, we provide special rates for long-term rentals. Reach out to our team for more details and to customize your booking.
