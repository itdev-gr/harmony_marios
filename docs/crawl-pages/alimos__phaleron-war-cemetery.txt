Phaleron War Cemetery of Alimos
Phaleron War Cemetery
The Phaleron War Cemetery, located in Alimos, Athens, is a solemn and beautifully maintained site honoring those who lost their lives during World War II. Managed by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, the cemetery serves as a poignant reminder of the sacrifices made by soldiers from various countries.
Surrounded by lush greenery and landscaped gardens, the cemetery provides a peaceful environment for reflection. Visitors can explore the meticulously kept graves and memorials, learning about the stories of the brave individuals who fought for freedom. The cemetery also offers a chance to connect with history, making it a significant destination for those interested in World War II heritage.
Situated near Alimos Beach and other local amenities, the Phaleron War Cemetery allows for a balanced visit combining historical exploration and leisure activities. Whether you are a history enthusiast, a relative of a soldier, or a traveler seeking meaningful experiences, the cemetery offers a place of contemplation and respect.

 Highlights of Phaleron War Cemetery of Alimos 
Historical Significance
Phaleron War Cemetery commemorates soldiers from World War II, providing a poignant glimpse into Greece’s role in the global conflict and honoring those who served.
Commonwealth War Graves Commission Management
The cemetery is meticulously maintained by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, ensuring respectful and well-preserved memorials for visitors to pay their respects.
Beautifully Landscaped Grounds
Set amidst lush greenery and manicured gardens, the cemetery offers a tranquil environment for reflection, making it a peaceful and respectful place to visit.
Memorial Monuments
The cemetery features several memorials dedicated to fallen soldiers, giving visitors the opportunity to learn about their stories and sacrifices during World War II.
Proximity to Alimos Beach
Located near the coastal area of Alimos, visitors can easily combine their visit with a trip to the beach, providing a balanced experience of history and relaxation.
Accessible by Public Transport
The cemetery is conveniently accessible via local public transport, ensuring ease of access for visitors coming from different parts of Athens.
Educational Experience
Guided tours and informational plaques provide educational insights into Greece’s wartime history, making it an enriching visit for history enthusiasts and students.
Close to Other Attractions
With its location in Alimos, the cinema is also close to other attractions like the Alimos Marina and local parks, making it an ideal spot for a well-rounded day of entertainment.
 Phaleron War Cemetery of Alimos Tour - Itinerary 
Arrival at Phaleron War Cemetery
