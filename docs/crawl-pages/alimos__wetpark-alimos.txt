WETPARK ALIMOS
WETPARK ALIMOS
Wetpark Alimos is a lively water park located along the beautiful Alimos coastline, offering a fun-filled experience for visitors of all ages. The park features a variety of water slides, wave pools, and lazy rivers, making it an ideal destination for families, friends, and adventure enthusiasts.
With well-maintained facilities and safety measures, Wetpark Alimos ensures a thrilling and secure environment. Visitors can enjoy a full day of aquatic activities, including kid-friendly play zones and high-speed slides for adrenaline seekers. The park also provides relaxation areas with sunbeds and umbrellas, allowing guests to unwind by the poolside.
Wetpark Alimos is conveniently situated near Alimos Beach and is easily accessible by public transport, making it a perfect spot to combine beach fun with an exciting day at the water park. Nearby restaurants and cafés offer a range of dining options, ensuring a delightful experience for everyone.

 Highlights of Wetpark Alimos 
Variety of Water Slides
Wetpark Alimos offers an array of exciting water slides, catering to all ages and thrill levels, from gentle slopes to high-speed plunges.
Wave Pools and Lazy Rivers
Enjoy the wave pools for a beach-like experience or float down the lazy rivers for a relaxing time in the water.
Kid-Friendly Play Zones
Special areas designed for children include shallow pools, mini slides, and interactive water play stations, ensuring fun and safety for younger visitors.
Relaxation Zones
For those looking to unwind, the park provides comfortable sunbeds and shaded umbrellas around the pools, creating a perfect spot to relax.
On-Site Dining Options
Wetpark Alimos features various food and beverage outlets offering snacks, meals, and refreshments to keep guests energized throughout the day.
Proximity to Alimos Beach
Located near Alimos Beach, the park offers the opportunity to combine a beach visit with the thrill of water rides, maximizing the coastal experience.
Safety and Lifeguard Services
The park is equipped with professional lifeguard services and safety measures, ensuring a secure environment for all guests.
 Wetpark Alimos Tour - Itinerary 
Pickup and Departure from Athens City Center
Start the day with a convenient pickup from your Athens hotel or a central meeting point. Enjoy a scenic drive to Wetpark Alimos.
Arrival and Welcome Briefing
Arrive at Wetpark Alimos and receive a short welcome briefing from the park’s staff. Learn about the attractions and safety measures for an exciting and secure experience.
Water Slides and Wave Pool Fun
Dive into the action with access to the park’s thrilling water slides and wave pools. Whether you seek adrenaline-pumping rides or gentle waves, there’s something for everyone.
Kids’ Zone Exploration
For families with children, explore the dedicated kids’ area featuring shallow pools, small slides, and interactive water play stations, perfect for younger visitors.
Lazy River and Relaxation Time
Float along the lazy river for a leisurely experience or unwind on the sunbeds by the poolside, soaking up the sun and enjoying the tranquil environment.
 Local Facilities for Wetpark Alimos Tour 
Convenient Parking Area
Wetpark Alimos offers ample parking space for visitors, ensuring easy access for those arriving by car or tour bus.
On-Site Restaurants and Snack Bars
Multiple dining options are available within the park, serving a variety of meals, snacks, and refreshments to keep guests energized throughout the day.
Changing Rooms and Lockers
The park provides clean changing rooms and secure locker facilities, allowing guests to store their belongings and prepare for water activities comfortably.
Sunbed and Umbrella Rentals
For those looking to relax, sunbeds and umbrellas are available for rent, offering shaded spots around the pools and lazy river for a comfortable experience.
Lifeguard and First Aid Services
Professional lifeguard services and a first aid station are present, ensuring the safety and well-being of all guests during their visit.
Kids’ Play Area
A specially designed kids’ zone is equipped with shallow pools, mini slides, and water play stations, providing a safe and fun environment for young children.
