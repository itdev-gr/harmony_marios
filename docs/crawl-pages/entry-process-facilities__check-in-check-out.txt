Check in & Check Out
Welcome to Your Home Away From Home!
Dear Guest,
I hope this message finds you well.
Thank you for choosing our property for your stay. We’re excited to host you and are confident you’ll enjoy your time with us!
To ensure a smooth check-in process and comply with Greek Government regulations, we kindly request the passport number of the individual under whom the booking is made.
Additionally, to help us coordinate our schedules and provide you with the best possible service, please inform us of your anticipated check-in and check-out times, as well as your flight number. This information will assist us in managing any unforeseen delays effectively.
Check-In/Out Details
Check-in
Check-in is available from 3:00 PM to 1:00 AM. For your convenience, we offer a self-check-in option available any time after 3:00 PM.
Check-out
Check-out must be completed by 11:00 AM to allow us to prepare for our next guests.
Check-In Requirment's
1)passport photo 
2) phone number 
3) email 
We also encourage you to familiarize yourself with our property rules to ensure a pleasant stay for everyone.
Should you have any questions or need further assistance, please don’t hesitate to reach out. We’re here to make your stay as enjoyable and comfortable as possible.
Thank you once again for choosing us. We look forward to welcoming you!
Best Regards,
Vasilis Evelina
Check-Out Instructions
Dear Guest,
I hope this message finds you well and that your stay has met all your expectations! As we prepare for your check-out tomorrow, could you kindly inform us of your planned departure time? Remember, check-out is by 11:00 AM.
– Please leave the keys on the table inside the apartment.
– *Important:* Ensure the keys are not left in the door upon leaving.
– Before departure, turn off all lights and the air conditioning, and kindly leave the apartment as tidy as you found it.
