Kinetta beach of Alimos
Kinetta Beach of Alimos Tour
Experience the beauty of Kinetta Beach, a serene coastal retreat located near Alimos, ideal for those seeking sun, sea, and relaxation. This pristine beach offers golden sands and crystal-clear waters, making it a perfect spot for swimming, sunbathing, and water activities.
Kinetta Beach is equipped with convenient amenities, including sunbeds, umbrellas, and beachfront cafes where you can enjoy delicious local cuisine while taking in the stunning sea views. It’s a great destination for families, couples, and groups of friends looking for a peaceful and picturesque beach day.
The area around Kinetta Beach also provides access to nearby attractions in Alimos, including vibrant markets, cultural sites, and additional beaches, ensuring a well-rounded experience for visitors. After spending a day soaking up the sun, you can explore Alimos further or relax with a seaside meal, making Kinetta Beach the perfect getaway for relaxation and exploration.

 Highlights of Kinetta Beach of Alimos Tour 
Stunning Coastal Beauty
Kinetta Beach features golden sands and clear turquoise waters, providing a perfect setting for swimming, sunbathing, and beach activities. It’s an ideal location for those seeking relaxation and a picturesque seaside experience.
Convenient Proximity to Attractions
Situated close to Alimos, Kinetta Beach offers easy access to various local attractions. Visitors can explore nearby markets, cultural landmarks, and other scenic beaches, creating an opportunity for a diverse and enriching experience.
Beachfront Amenities
The beach is equipped with essential amenities, including sunbeds, umbrellas, and beachfront cafes, where visitors can enjoy refreshments and local delicacies while soaking in the coastal views.
Family-Friendly Environment
Kinetta Beach is suitable for families, offering safe swimming conditions and plenty of space for children to play. Its welcoming atmosphere ensures that visitors of all ages can enjoy a day at the beach.
Easy Access to Alimos Beaches
Kinetta Beach’s proximity to other popular beaches in Alimos allows visitors to explore multiple seaside spots in one day, making it easy to experience the best of the coastal area.
Local Dining Options
After a relaxing beach day, visitors can easily access nearby restaurants and cafes that serve fresh seafood and traditional Greek cuisine, providing an authentic culinary experience right by the sea.
 Kinetta Beach of Alimos Tour - Itinerary 
Pickup from Your Alimos Accommodation
Start your beach adventure with a convenient pickup from your hotel or a central meeting point in Alimos, ensuring a comfortable journey to Kinetta Beach.
Arrival at Kinetta Beach
Arrive at the beautiful Kinetta Beach, where you’ll be greeted by golden sands and turquoise waters. Settle into your beach spot with sunbeds and umbrellas provided.
Free Time for Swimming and Relaxation
Enjoy a morning of leisure by the sea—take a refreshing swim, sunbathe on the soft sands, or relax under the shade of an umbrella.
Lunch at a Beachfront Cafe
Head to a beachfront cafe to savor a delicious lunch with fresh local cuisine. Enjoy traditional Greek dishes while admiring the stunning sea views.
Optional Water Activities
After lunch, you can choose to participate in water sports or activities such as paddleboarding, snorkeling, or beach volleyball, perfect for those seeking some adventure.
Explore the Local Area
Take a stroll along the beach or explore nearby attractions. You may choose to visit other beaches in Alimos or check out local shops and cafes for a complete seaside experience.
 Local Facilities for Kinetta Beach of Alimos Tour 
Beachfront Cafes and Restaurants
Kinetta Beach offers a range of beachfront cafes and restaurants where visitors can enjoy fresh seafood, traditional Greek dishes, and refreshing beverages, all with stunning sea views.
Sunbeds and Umbrellas
Visitors can rent sunbeds and umbrellas, ensuring a comfortable and relaxing beach experience. These facilities are conveniently available throughout the beach, allowing for easy access.
Water Sports and Equipment Rentals
For those seeking adventure, Kinetta Beach provides facilities for water sports like paddleboarding and snorkeling. Equipment rentals are available on-site for a convenient and fun experience.
Public Restrooms and Showers
Clean and well-maintained restrooms and shower facilities are accessible for visitors, ensuring a comfortable and hygienic beach visit.
 Parking Facilities
Ample parking spaces are available near Kinetta Beach, making it easy for visitors to park their vehicles close to the beach area, providing hassle-free access.
