Kalamaki Beach of Alimos
Kalamaki Beach of Alimos
Kalamaki Beach in Alimos is the go-to spot for those looking to unwind by the sea. Picture soft golden sands, clear turquoise waters, and a laid-back vibe perfect for soaking up the sun or taking a dip. Whether you want to lounge on a sunbed with an umbrella or dive into some exciting watersports, there’s something for everyone here.
The beach is surrounded by lively beach bars and cozy restaurants, so you can easily grab a bite of local cuisine or enjoy a refreshing drink without straying far from the shore. Plus, with its close proximity to Athens, Kalamaki Beach is a convenient escape for anyone craving some coastal relaxation or adventure.

 Highlights of Kalamaki Beach of Alimos 
Stunning Sandy Shoreline
Kalamaki Beach boasts a golden sandy shoreline and crystal-clear waters, providing a perfect spot for sunbathing, swimming, or simply relaxing by the sea.
Proximity to Vibrant Beach Bars and Restaurants
Just steps away from the beach, visitors can find a range of lively bars and cozy restaurants offering local cuisine and refreshing beverages, making it easy to enjoy a meal or drink without leaving the beachfront.
Variety of Watersports
The beach offers numerous watersport activities like paddleboarding, jet skiing, and windsurfing, catering to both thrill-seekers and those wanting a leisurely adventure on the water.
Easy Access to Nearby Attractions
Kalamaki Beach is conveniently located close to popular attractions in Alimos and Athens, allowing visitors to easily combine beach time with sightseeing tours of historic and cultural sites.
Family-Friendly Amenities
With sunbeds, umbrellas, and play areas, the beach caters to families looking for a safe and enjoyable day out, ensuring a comfortable experience for visitors of all ages.
Scenic Coastal Pathways
The area surrounding Kalamaki Beach features scenic pathways perfect for leisurely strolls or bike rides, allowing visitors to explore the beauty of Alimos and its coastal charm.
Nearby Shopping and Convenience Stores
Conveniently located shops and stores are available nearby, offering beach essentials, snacks, and drinks to ensure visitors have everything they need for a day by the sea.
 Itineraries 
Convenient Pickup from Your Alimos Accommodation
Start your day with a quick pickup from your hotel in Alimos, setting the stage for a relaxing beach outing.
Arrive at the Beautiful Kalamaki Beach
Get ready to unwind as you arrive at Kalamaki Beach. Choose your spot on the soft sands or grab a sunbed to kick back and soak up the sun.
Morning of Beach Activities and Relaxation
Enjoy a leisurely morning swimming in the inviting waters or participating in thrilling watersports like paddleboarding and jet skiing, perfect for all adventure lovers.
Free Time for Shopping and Exploration
Take advantage of nearby shops and convenience stores for souvenirs or essentials. Explore the local cafes and bars to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere.
Departure
Your guide will assist in organizing transportation back to your accommodation or help you explore other nightlife options nearby.
 Local Facilities at Kalamaki Beach of Alimos 
Sunbeds and Umbrellas
Kalamaki Beach offers a range of comfortable sunbeds and umbrellas available for rent, providing the perfect setup for a day of relaxation under the sun.
Beachfront Restaurants and Cafés
Several beachfront dining options serve delicious local cuisine and refreshing drinks. Enjoy a meal with stunning views of the sea, ensuring a delightful dining experience.
Watersport Rentals
For adventure enthusiasts, the beach features rentals for various watersports, including paddleboarding, jet skiing, and windsurfing. Equipment is readily available for both beginners and experienced thrill-seekers.
Showers and Changing Facilities
Convenient shower facilities and changing rooms are available on-site, allowing visitors to freshen up after swimming or enjoying the beach activities.
Lifeguard Services
Kalamaki Beach is monitored by lifeguards, ensuring a safe environment for swimming and other activities, providing peace of mind for families and beachgoers.
