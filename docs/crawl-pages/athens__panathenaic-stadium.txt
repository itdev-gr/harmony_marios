Panathenaic Stadium of Athens
The Panathenaic Stadium, also known as Kallimarmaro, is one of Athens’ most iconic and historic landmarks. As the only stadium in the world built entirely of marble, it offers a glimpse into the grandeur of ancient Greek sports and architecture. Originally constructed in the 4th century BC, it was later renovated by the Romans and served as the venue for the first modern Olympic Games in 1896.
Visitors can walk through the stadium’s impressive stands, explore the track where ancient athletes once competed, and visit the museum showcasing memorabilia from the modern Olympics. With stunning views of Athens, the Panathenaic Stadium is a must-visit for sports enthusiasts and history lovers alike, celebrating the legacy of athleticism and competition that dates back thousands of years.

 Panathenaic Stadium of Athens Highlights 
Historic Venue for the Olympics
The Panathenaic Stadium hosted the first modern Olympic Games in 1896, making it a significant site in the history of international sports.
Only All-Marble Stadium
As the world’s only stadium built entirely of marble, the Panathenaic Stadium showcases the architectural excellence and grandeur of ancient Greece.
Ancient Origins
Originally constructed in the 4th century BC, this stadium offers a direct connection to the athletic competitions and traditions of ancient Athens.
Walk the Ancient Track
Visitors can walk the very track where ancient athletes once competed, offering an immersive experience of Greece’s athletic heritage.
Cultural Events and Concerts
The stadium continues to host events and concerts, blending ancient history with contemporary culture and entertainment.
Convenient Location
Situated in the heart of Athens, the Panathenaic Stadium is easily accessible and close to other major attractions, making it an ideal stop on your Athens tour.
 Itineraries 
Welcome and Introduction
Meet at the entrance of the Panathenaic Stadium. Your guide will provide a brief overview of the site’s historical and cultural significance before beginning the tour.
Explore the Marble Stands
Ascend the marble steps and take in the panoramic views of Athens from the stadium’s stands. Learn about its unique architectural features as the only all-marble stadium in the world.
Walk the Ancient Track
Experience the thrill of walking on the very track where ancient and modern athletes have competed. Your guide will share stories of the athletic traditions that originated here.
Visit the Museum of the Modern Olympics
Discover the history of the modern Olympic Games through memorabilia and artifacts displayed at the museum. Gain insights into the stadium’s role in reviving the Olympics in 1896.
Photo Opportunities
Take some time for photographs with the stadium as a backdrop, capturing its majestic marble architecture and the surrounding Athenian landscape.
Free Exploration and Relaxation
Enjoy a brief period of free time to explore the stadium at your own pace. You can revisit key areas, rest, or take in the views of Athens from the stands.

Accessibility
The Panathenaic Stadium offers accessible entrances and pathways, ensuring that visitors of all abilities can enjoy exploring this iconic site comfortably.
Restroom Facilities
Clean and well-maintained restrooms are available near the entrance, providing convenience throughout your visit.
Information Center
An information center is located at the entrance, offering brochures, maps, and staff assistance for inquiries about the stadium and other nearby attractions.
Café and Refreshment Stand
A nearby café provides a selection of drinks and snacks, offering a perfect spot for a quick refreshment break during your visit.
