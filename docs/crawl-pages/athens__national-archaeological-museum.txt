National Archaeological Museum of Athens
Embark on a journey through ancient Greek history at the National Archaeological Museum of Athens, one of the most renowned museums in the world. Housing an extensive collection of artifacts from various periods, including the Mycenaean, Cycladic, and Classical eras, this museum showcases Greece’s artistic and cultural evolution. Our expert-guided tour provides fascinating insights into the sculptures, pottery, jewelry, and frescoes that illustrate ancient life and beliefs. Highlights include the Mask of Agamemnon, the Antikythera Mechanism, and iconic statues like the Poseidon of Artemision. Experience Greece’s rich heritage as you explore these remarkable treasures and gain a deeper appreciation of its timeless influence on art and civilization.

 National Archaeological Museum of Athens Highlights 
Greece’s Largest Museum
The National Archaeological Museum is Greece’s most comprehensive museum, featuring an extensive collection of artifacts spanning from prehistoric times to late antiquity, showcasing Greece’s rich cultural heritage.
Close to Major Attractions
Centrally located in Athens, the museum is near other key sites like the Acropolis, Syntagma Square, and the lively Monastiraki district, making it an ideal stop on your Athens itinerary.
Access to Beautiful Beaches
A short drive from the museum, visitors can relax at Athens’ picturesque beaches such as Alimos and Glyfada, perfect for unwinding after exploring the city’s historical landmarks.
Nearby Dining and Shopping
Surrounding the museum are numerous cafés, traditional Greek restaurants, and shops where visitors can enjoy local cuisine, refreshments, and shop for authentic souvenirs.
Accessible Public Transport
The museum is well-connected by metro, buses, and taxis, providing convenient transportation options for reaching other attractions and neighborhoods across the city.
Panoramic Garden Area
The museum’s outdoor garden offers a peaceful spot to relax and enjoy the surroundings, providing a break between exploring the extensive collection of exhibits.
Proximity to Historic Plaka District
Just a short walk from the museum, the Plaka district offers a charming experience with its narrow streets, neoclassical architecture, and vibrant atmosphere, perfect for further exploration.
 Itineraries 
Meet & Greet
Start your adventure by gathering at the entrance of the National Archaeological Museum. Our knowledgeable guide will welcome you and outline the tour’s highlights.
Museum Introduction
Begin with a fascinating introduction to the museum’s rich history. Discover how it became a vital institution for preserving Greece’s ancient artifacts since its establishment in the 19th century.
Prehistoric Collection
Delve into the Prehistoric Collection, where you’ll encounter artifacts from the Cycladic Islands and Mycenaean civilization. Gain insights into the artistic styles and everyday life of these early cultures.
Sculptures and Statues
Explore the gallery showcasing iconic ancient Greek sculptures, including the “Kouros” and the “Venus of Milo.” Your guide will share captivating stories behind these masterpieces and their significance in Greek art.
Mycenaean Gold and Jewelry
Admire stunning gold artifacts and jewelry from the Mycenaean period, including the legendary “Mask of Agamemnon.” Learn about the intricate craftsmanship and the symbolism behind these exquisite treasures.
The Antikythera Mechanism
Discover the Antikythera Mechanism, an ancient analog computer that illustrates Greece’s advanced scientific achievements. Understand its historical context and importance in the development of technology.
The Bronze Age Gallery
Explore the Bronze Age Gallery, where artifacts highlight the technological advancements and cultural practices of ancient civilizations. Uncover the innovations that shaped the era.
nd of Tour & Recommendations
Conclude your tour with helpful recommendations from your guide for further exploration in Athens. Learn about nearby attractions, dining options, and unique shops to enhance your visit.
 Local Facility for Acropolis Museum of Athens 
Accessibility
The National Archaeological Museum is equipped with ramps and elevators, ensuring easy access for all visitors, including those with mobility challenges.
 On-Site Café
Enjoy a break at the museum’s café, which offers a selection of refreshments and light snacks, perfect for recharging during your visit.
Gift Shop
Visit the museum’s gift shop for a variety of souvenirs, books, and replicas of famous artifacts, allowing you to take a piece of history home.
Information Desk
The information desk provides assistance with inquiries about the museum, tours, and local attractions, ensuring a smooth experience during your visit.
Nearby Public Transport
The museum is easily accessible via public transport, with nearby metro stations and bus stops, making it convenient for visitors to explore Athens further.
Parking Facilities
Limited parking is available in the vicinity of the museum, providing options for those traveling by car.
Nearby Attractions
After your tour, explore nearby attractions such as the Omonia Square, Exarchia district, and the stunning National Garden of Athens, all within walking distance.
