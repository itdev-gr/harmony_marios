Main Door:
 The photo above shows the main door of the building.

Take the 
elevator
 or the

 to the 
6th floor
.

 The elevator has a 
weight limit of 3 people

Apartment Entrance Instructions
Location:
 On the 
6th floor
, as you exit the elevator, the door is on your 
left-hand side
 (see photo above).
Security System:
 The door is equipped with a digital lock.
How to Enter:
Enter the 
6-digit code
 that will be sent to you via WhatsApp 
1 hour before your check-in time
.
After entering the code, press the 
bell icon
 (bottom-right of the keypad).
The door will unlock.
Important:
This procedure is required only for the 
first entry
 (check-in).
After entering, you can take the keys from the 
inside magnetic lock
 (photo will follow) to lock and unlock the door during your stay.
Always use the key for extra security for your belongings.
Hot Water Instructions
Location:
 The switch for the water heater is located 
in the hallway between the two rooms
, next to the apartment’s electrical panel (on the left side of the panel).
How to Use:
Press the switch (see photo above).
The water heater will automatically start heating the water.
Wait for 
30-40 minutes
, and the hot water will be ready.
Note:
 The water heater is controlled by a timer, so you don’t need to turn it off manually. It will stop heating automatically.
