 Frequently Asked Questions
What is the average rent for an apartment in Athens?
The average rent for a one-bedroom apartment in the city center is typically between €700 and €1,100 per month. In neighborhoods outside the center, prices can range from €500 to €800. Prices vary significantly based on location, size, and amenities.
Is Athens a safe city to live in?
Yes, Athens is considered a very safe city. While it's always wise to take standard precautions, neighborhoods like Kolonaki, Kifisia, and Glyfada are particularly known for their safety and are popular choices for families and single residents.
Is Athens a good place for expats to live?
Absolutely. With its affordable cost of living, vibrant culture, and large, welcoming international community, Athens is an excellent city for expats. The city offers a unique blend of ancient history and modern energy, providing a high quality of life for those who choose to call it home.
